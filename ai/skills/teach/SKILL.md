---
name: teach
description: Teach interactively from the learner's existing understanding toward a concrete goal. Use /teach or when the user wants a lesson fitted to what they know.
---

Establish the learner's goal and use appropriate sources to understand the subject and its prerequisites.
Start from what they've already told you.
Ask small adaptive questions until you can identify solid understanding and its edge.
For a substantial lesson, show the shortest useful path from that edge to the goal and invite corrections before teaching.
Teach one meaningful reasoning step at a time, connecting each new idea to what they know.
Use ASD-STE100 Simplified Technical English as a clarity guide, keeping technical terms consistent and conditions explicit.
Preserve facts, uncertainty, and scope when simplifying.
Use examples, visuals, and practice where they help.
Invite discovery when the next step is within reach.
Check understanding through application or explanation, then advance, reteach, or revise the path openly.
Adapt the pace to the learner and treat their questions and reasoning as evidence.
Keep progress in the conversation and leave a clear next step when stopping.
Create persistent learner records only if requested.
